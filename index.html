🔍 How Sunny works

Data & AI transparency — written to be readable by a teacher, a principal, or a parent.

📥 What we collect

One tap per check-in. That is the entire dataset.

  • A weather choice — one of 5 values, stored as a number 1–5
  • An optional reason tag — one of 6 fixed options, including “rather not say”
  • A timestamp and the class ID
  • No free text — a child can never type something we can't review
  • No camera, microphone, face or voice analysis of any kind
  • No behavioural tracking, no keystrokes, no location

⚙️ How a signal is produced

1 · Input5 numbers (this week) + a 30-day median for this child
2 · DetectionThree fixed arithmetic rules. Deterministic and auditable. No AI.
3 · WordingAI turns a fired rule into a plain-English suggestion for the teacher
4 · DecisionThe teacher decides. Always. Sunny never contacts anyone.

The AI never decides who gets flagged. It receives only the rule name and the five numbers — no name, no reason tags, no history — and returns a sentence. Remove the AI entirely and Sunny still flags exactly the same children.

📐 The three rules, in full

R1Sudden change — the child's check-in sits ≥2 steps below their own 30-day median for 2 or more consecutive days.
R2Sustained drift — ≥1.5 steps below their own median for 3 or more consecutive days.
R3Repeating cycle — the same weekday falls ≥1.5 steps below their own median in ≥4 of the last 6 occurrences.

Every rule compares a child only to themselves. A child who is consistently cloudy is never flagged for being cloudy — that is their normal, and normal is not a problem. This is deliberate: it protects quiet and neurodivergent kids from being pathologised for their baseline.

🚫 What Sunny never does

  • Diagnose, or suggest any clinical or developmental label
  • Rank or compare children against each other, or against a class average
  • Infer why a child feels something — it cannot know, and never claims to
  • Notify parents, counsellors or anyone else automatically
  • Train any model on children's data, or send it to a third party
  • Keep raw check-ins beyond 30 days — older data is deleted, not archived

👀 Who sees what

  • The child — their own history, on request. Nothing is hidden from them.
  • The class teacher — their own class only
  • The wellbeing lead — only when a teacher chooses to escalate
  • Parents — only via the school's existing process, never automatically

A teacher can dismiss any signal. Dismissals are kept so we can measure the false-positive rate honestly and publish it.
