# Team Summary

**Team:** Team Sunny · **Project:** Sunny — Classroom Mood Check-in · **Domain:** Education Technology

**Members:** Arthur Chaves (300006351) · Ayra Inciong (111580) · Ben Harley (111816)

---

Children in distress — especially those who mask, or are neurodivergent and find
feelings hard to name — go unnoticed until things escalate. One teacher cannot read
twenty-six inner states at once.

Sunny is a twenty-second daily check-in. Children tap one of five faces — no password,
no vocabulary needed. Five transparent arithmetic rules then compare each child only to
their own thirty-day baseline, never to classmates, and surface soft signals: a sudden
drop, a sustained drift, a repeating weekday pattern, a child who has stopped answering,
or one tapping without reading.

Detection is deterministic and auditable — a parent could check the maths. Generative AI
only phrases the suggestion a teacher reads; it never decides who is flagged. Support
plans written by parents and teachers outrank it.

Expected impact: earlier, kinder intervention for the children hardest to see, less
teacher load, and an AI a school can defend to parents.
