# Team Summary

**Team:** [TEAM NAME] · **Project:** Sunny — Classroom Mood Check-in · **Domain:** Education Technology

---

Children in distress — especially those who mask, or who are neurodivergent and find
feelings hard to name — go unnoticed until things escalate. One teacher cannot read
twenty-six inner states at once.

Sunny is a twenty-second daily check-in. Children tap one of five faces, no password and
no vocabulary required. Four transparent arithmetic rules then compare each child only to
their own thirty-day baseline, never to classmates, and surface soft signals: a sudden
drop, a sustained drift, a repeating weekday pattern, or a child who has stopped answering
altogether.

Detection is deterministic and auditable — a parent could check the maths. Generative AI
only phrases the suggestion a teacher reads; it never decides who is flagged. Support plans
written by parents and teachers rank above the machine's suggestion.

Expected impact: earlier and kinder intervention for the children who are hardest to see,
less load on teachers, and an AI a school can actually defend to parents.
